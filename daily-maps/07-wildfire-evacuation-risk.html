What the fusion means
A county ranks higher when its representative interior point is close to a current mapped wildfire, the nearby incident is large and less contained, and the county has more people or higher CDC Social Vulnerability Index. NWS fire-weather alerts add context where an official alert polygon covers the county point. To keep the browser responsive, CDC counties are requested only around current wildfire areas instead of downloading every U.S. county polygon.

Sources & refresh

NIFC WFIGS Current Interagency Fire Perimeters: anonymous ArcGIS FeatureServer, wildfire features only; checked every 10 minutes. Perimeter timestamps and incident acreage/containment are source attributes.

National Weather Service Alerts API: active Red Flag Warnings and Fire Weather Watches; checked every 5 minutes.

CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022 county layer: county geometry, ACS-derived population and overall SVI rank. Static 2022 vintage. Spatially bounded queries load only counties near current wildfire perimeters and are cached until the fire-area grid changes.

U.S. Census TIGERweb Transportation: primary/secondary roads rendered by an anonymous public MapServer; static/current TIGERweb context, loaded only when toggled.

Omitted: national evacuation orders and road closures. No authoritative, consistently structured, anonymous nationwide browser endpoint was verified, so the map does not invent or infer official evacuation zones or closures.
