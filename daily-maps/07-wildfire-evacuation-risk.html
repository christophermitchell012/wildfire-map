How the score works
Hazard, max 75
distance: 45 / 35 / 25 / 15 at ≤2 / 10 / 25 / 50 km
fire size: min(15, 3 × log10(acres + 1))
uncontained: 15 × (1 − containment/100)

Exposure, max 10
population: min(10, 2 × log10(population))

Vulnerability, max 15
SVI: 15 × overall SVI rank

Context
NWS Red Flag / Fire Weather Watch: displayed separately as +10 context; total capped at 100. Distance uses a representative county interior point.
Loading & reliability
Regional queries reduce geometry. Live requests use timeouts, retry transient failures with exponential backoff, cache the last successful regional response in the browser, prevent overlapping requests and pause scheduled refresh while hidden or offline. A cached response is labeled Cached, never Live.
Sources & refresh
NIFC WFIGS Current Interagency Fire Perimeters: selected-region query; 10 min.

National Weather Service Alerts API: active fire-weather products; 5 min.

CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022: county population and SVI; static 2022 vintage.

U.S. Census TIGERweb Transportation: 2025 primary/secondary roads; context only, not closures or evacuation routes.

Omitted: nationwide evacuation orders and road closures because no authoritative, consistently structured, anonymous nationwide browser endpoint was verified.
