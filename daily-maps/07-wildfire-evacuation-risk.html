Sources & refresh

NIFC WFIGS Current Interagency Fire Perimeters: anonymous ArcGIS FeatureServer; selected-region query; checked every 10 minutes.

National Weather Service Alerts API: national active feed filtered to fire-weather products intersecting the selected region; checked every 5 minutes.

CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022 county layer: selected-region county geometry, ACS-derived population and overall SVI rank; static 2022 vintage, loaded once per region selection.

U.S. Census TIGERweb Transportation: Primary Roads layer 0 and Secondary Roads layer 1, January 1, 2025 vintage. Queried only for the selected region when enabled. Primary roads are cyan and secondary roads amber for contrast; these are access context, not closures or official evacuation routes.

Omitted: national evacuation orders and road closures because no authoritative, consistently structured, anonymous nationwide browser endpoint was verified.
