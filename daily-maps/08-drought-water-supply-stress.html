Three drought lenses
Meteorological/agricultural: USDM D0–D4 is the official broad-scale weekly synthesis and incorporates many indicators and impacts.

Hydrological: USGS points show current discharge in ft³/s. They are operational observations, not labeled low-flow without a historical percentile comparison.

Exposure/vulnerability: CDC SVI and population show where drought coincides with communities potentially less able to absorb disruption.

Sources & cadence

U.S. Drought Monitor current FeatureServer: NDMC/USDA/NOAA current D0–D4 polygons. Weekly product; loaded once per selected region. The service exposes release/map dates.

USGS Instantaneous Values Service: parameter 00060, discharge in ft³/s. Browser CORS is supported by USGS. Checked every 30 minutes while visible/online, spatially split into ≤5°×5° cells because USGS limits bounding-box requests to 25 square degrees.

CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022 county layer: county geometry, ACS-derived population, overall SVI percentile rank. Static 2022 vintage; loaded once per selected region.

Omitted: reservoir storage, groundwater percentile, snowpack/SWE and precipitation anomaly are not included in this version because a single nationwide browser-safe/keyless source with comparable current values was not verified during this build. Their absence is shown rather than replaced with synthetic data.
