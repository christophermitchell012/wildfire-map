Derived score
County centroid screening only: risk + alert + population, capped at 100. SPC risk contributes 0/18/30/45/62/78 for none/marginal/slight/enhanced/moderate/high; tornado probability adds up to 12 points; an active Severe Thunderstorm Warning adds 12 and Tornado Warning adds 25; population contributes min(15, 3×log10(population)). Watches add 5. A county can be missed when only part of it intersects a polygon because the test uses the Census internal point/centroid, not area-weighted population.
Sources & cadence
NOAA/NWS Storm Prediction Center outlook service: Day 1 scheduled at 0100, 0600, 1300, 1630, 2000Z; checked every 15 min.

NWS Alerts API: active watches/warnings; checked every 2 min, slower than NWS's 30-second minimum guidance.

NWS Local Storm Reports: last 24 hours; upstream refresh about every 30 min; checked every 30 min.

2024 ACS 5-year population + 2025 TIGERweb generalized counties: static for this page session.
