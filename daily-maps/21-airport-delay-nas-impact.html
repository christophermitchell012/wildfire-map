How to read this map

This is an airport-system impact map, not a live aircraft-position tracker and not a flight-specific delay predictor. Red rings indicate FAA Ground Delay Programs. Orange rings indicate airports listed for general arrival/departure delays. Green points are selected major airports absent from the FAA national major-impact summary at snapshot time. Absence means “not listed for a major impact” only, not “zero delay.”

Method

Screening class is categorical, not a numeric MitchellCo risk score: Ground Delay Program = high system-management impact; General Arrival/Departure Delays = elevated; not listed = no major impact reported in the national summary. No missing value is converted to zero. Airport coordinates are static reference coordinates rounded to roughly airport location.

Sources & vintage

FAA ATCSCC National Airport Status Summary, snapshot checked Sep 19, 2026 9:24 AM CT. The FAA states this page lists major U.S. airports experiencing delays or affected by traffic-management initiatives and that status is not flight-specific.
