How the derived score works
Event hazard, max 55
magnitude contribution = clamp(0,55,20 × (M − 2.5))
shallow-depth bonus = max(0,10 × (1 − depth/50 km))
event hazard capped at 55

Distance attenuation
R = clamp(30,300,20 × 2^(M − 3)) km
attenuation = exp(−distance/R)

Exposure, max 25
min(25, 4.2 × log10(population + 1))

Vulnerability, max 20
20 × overall SVI percentile rank

Concern
attenuation × (hazard + exposure + vulnerability), capped at 100.
Refresh & reliability
USGS's seven-day GeoJSON feed refreshes every 2 minutes. CDC SVI is static 2022 vintage and loads only on region changes. Broad raw responses are not stored in localStorage. Only normalized state population totals use versioned 15-day localStorage entries. Cache access/write failure is non-fatal. CDC and ShakeMap failures cannot cancel the earthquake feed or other UI work. Retries are bounded exponential backoff for transient/network failures only.
