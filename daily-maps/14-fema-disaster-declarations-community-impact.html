DisasterWatch: FEMA Declarations & Community Impact

How to read this map
Hazard / event: recent FEMA federal disaster declarations and incident type.
Exposure: CDC/ATSDR population represented by counties with valid 2022 SVI values, aggregated to state level.
Consequence / vulnerability: FEMA assistance programs, declared-area breadth, and mean county SVI percentile.

FEMA and CDC values are official source data. The MitchellCo attention score and marker placement are non-official screening aids.
Where are recent federal disaster declarations broadest, and where do they overlap larger or more vulnerable populations?
Cached FEMA state summaries may paint immediately on repeat visits, then OpenFEMA revalidates in the background. CDC 2022 state aggregates are cached separately because the vintage is frozen. Markers use representative state coordinates, not incident locations.

Derived attention score
declaration = max(DR 30, EM 22, FM 14, FS 10)
recency multiplier = 0.65 + 0.35 × max(0, 1 − newestAgeDays/windowDays)
hazard = declaration × recency multiplier, max 30
scope = min(25, 8 × log2(distinctDeclaredAreas + 1))
assistance = min(15, 6 if IA/IH + 5 if PA + 4 if HM)
exposure = min(15, 2.15 × log10(representedPopulation + 1))
vulnerability = 15 × meanCountySVIPercentile
attention = hazard + scope + assistance + exposure + vulnerability, capped 100
Population is state context, not an estimate of people affected. Mean county SVI is not population-weighted. The score does not estimate damages, casualties, eligibility, or unmet need.
Score edge cases checked
Zero declarations produces no score. One recent DR area has only 8/25 scope points. Two areas produce about 12.7/25. A record as old as the selected window receives 65% of its declaration-type points. A recent DR with broad scope, all assistance categories, large represented population, and high mean SVI reaches the 100 cap without any component exceeding its published maximum.
Refresh & performance
OpenFEMA refreshes every 20 minutes. The first FEMA page renders immediately; if more pages exist they load concurrently with a maximum concurrency of three. Repeat visits can paint a compact cached state summary before revalidation. CDC/ATSDR SVI is 2022 vintage; its small state aggregate is cached for 30 days. Cache failures never invalidate live data. Automatic refresh pauses while hidden or offline and never resets pan, zoom, layer state, or popups. Basemap tile updates wait until map movement is idle and retain only a one-tile buffer.
