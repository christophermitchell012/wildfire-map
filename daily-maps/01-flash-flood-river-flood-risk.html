RiverWatch: Flash Flood & River Flood Risk

What this map combines

Hazard: USGS river measurements and official NWS flood alerts. Exposure: county population. Vulnerability: CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index (SVI). Gauge colors use a transparent, non-official derived score to prioritize attention.

score = alert points + stage-rise points + discharge-rise points + SVI points alert: Flood Advisory 20, Flood Watch 25, Flood Warning 50, Flash Flood Warning 65 stage rise: min(20, max(0, Δgage_ft_6h × 10)) discharge rise: min(10, max(0, %Δflow_6h / 10)) SVI: 10 × county overall SVI percentile rank final score capped at 100

This is not an NWS or USGS flood forecast. It does not use official flood-stage thresholds, which vary by gauge. Always follow official warnings and local emergency management.

Refresh / vintage

USGS instantaneous values: checked hourly, consistent with USGS guidance that hourly polling is usually sufficient for IV data. NWS active alerts: checked every 5 minutes, slower than the NWS maximum recommended request frequency. CDC SVI: 2022 dataset, loaded once per area/view and cached. NWS quantitative precipitation: loaded only when requested in a gauge popup.
