What this map combines

Hazard: USGS river measurements and official NWS flood alerts. Exposure: county population. Vulnerability: CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index (SVI). Gauge colors use a transparent, non-official derived score to prioritize attention.

This is not an NWS or USGS flood forecast. The derived score does not use official flood-stage threshold values. Where available, a gauge popup can load the official NWS Action/Minor/Moderate/Major thresholds separately from the public NWPS API. Always follow official warnings and local emergency management.

Refresh / vintage

USGS current conditions: checked every 15 minutes from the National Water Dashboard CWIS current-conditions service. Regional/Continental presets split the USGS request into four spatial tiles and merge the results; the UI reports if a tile hits the 5,000-row cap. If CWIS is unavailable, RiverWatch falls back to the modern USGS latest-continuous OGC API and caches that fallback for one hour. NWS active alerts: checked every 5 minutes. CDC SVI: 2022 dataset, loaded once per area/view and cached. NWS precipitation and official NWPS flood-category thresholds load only when requested in a gauge popup.

Sources

USGS National Water Dashboard / Water Data APIs
National Water Dashboard · Modern Water Data APIs
Primary source: anonymous CWIS CurrentConditions OData used by the National Water Dashboard, spatially bounded to the selected state area. Parameters: 00060 discharge, 00065 gage height, 00010 water temperature. It also supplies rate-of-change and official day-of-year streamflow status where available. Fallback: anonymous modern latest-continuous OGC API, queried at low frequency without an API key. The legacy waterservices.usgs.gov IV endpoint was removed from RiverWatch after returning HTTP 403 during USGS's 2026 migration/degradation period.

National Weather Service APIs
weather.gov alerts / forecast API · NWPS hydrologic API
Active CAP/GeoJSON flood alerts plus on-demand point/grid quantitative precipitation forecast. Gauge popups can also load official NWS flood-category thresholds (Action, Minor, Moderate, Major) from NWPS using the USGS site ID. Flood stages are maintained by NWS and are not established for every USGS streamgage. Open U.S. government data; no key/account.

CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index 2022
CDC OneMap county FeatureServer
Overall county SVI rank (RPL_THEMES) and estimated total population (E_TOTPOP), queried as GeoJSON for the current map area.

Basemaps: six keyless choices matching the wildfire map. USGS Imagery Topo, USGS Topo, and USGS Shaded Relief are from The National Map. NASA GIBS Blue Marble is from NASA EOSDIS and caps at zoom 8. OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap are community tile services under their own attribution and usage policies.

Preferred layer omitted: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer is not shown in this build because an anonymous browser/CORS path was not verified strongly enough during this run. No floodplain geometry is fabricated or substituted.
