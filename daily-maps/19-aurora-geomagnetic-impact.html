How to read this map

Colored cells are NOAA's OVATION short-term aurora forecast. Kp is NOAA's planetary geomagnetic activity index. The map does not claim ground-level infrastructure damage. Higher geomagnetic activity can affect radio, navigation, satellite and power-system operations, but actual impacts depend on location, system design and event duration.
