How the score works
Weather hazard, max 40
max active NWS extreme-weather product intersecting the control area, weighted by event type/severity.

Historical operating margin, max 35
margin = (available capacity − peak load) / peak load. Lower or negative legacy margin scores higher.

Population exposure, max 15
min(15, 2.5 × log10(population assigned by county interior points)).

Social vulnerability, max 10
10 × population-weighted mean CDC SVI rank.

Total = sum, capped at 100. This is a non-official screening index. Historical capacity/load values are not current operating conditions.
Time-series view
Click a ranked control area or shaded polygon to load an on-demand 48-hour Open-Meteo temperature/apparent-temperature forecast at that area's representative point. The sparkline is weather context only and is not silently converted into actual electricity demand.
Loading & reliability
Live requests use timeouts, bounded spatial queries, browser caching, overlap protection and retry/backoff. Scheduled NWS refresh pauses while the page is hidden or offline. Optional transmission geometry loads only when enabled.
Sources, vintages & omissions
National Weather Service Alerts API: active extreme heat/cold, severe storm, high-wind, ice and winter-weather products; refresh every 5 minutes; zone-based alerts are resolved to official NWS zone geometry.

Electric Control Areas / Balancing Areas: static GIS layer with legacy available-capacity, peak-load and total-capacity fields; source layer last edited 2021. Loaded once per region.

CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022: county population and overall SVI rank; static 2022 vintage.

Archived U.S. electric transmission lines: HIFLD-derived 2023/2024-era public copy, no longer updated; optional context only.

Open-Meteo Forecast API: keyless, CORS-enabled 48-hour hourly forecast requested on demand at the selected control-area point; cache 30 minutes; attribution CC BY 4.0.

Omitted: live EIA balancing-authority demand/generation. EIA's API requires a registered API key, which violates this map series' no-key rule. Therefore this map must not be interpreted as live reserve margin, generation adequacy, or outage risk.
