Hazard / signal: NOAA high-tide-flood likelihood and NWS coastal alerts
Exposure: coastal county population
Consequence / vulnerability: CDC/ATSDR SVI

Official/source values remain distinct from MitchellCo derived screening scores.

How the score works
Derived Coastal Concern, 0–100
NOAA HTF likelihood: 0.60 × likelihood %, max 60
NWS coastal alert at station point: Warning 20, Advisory 12, Watch 8
County vulnerability: 12 × SVI percentile rank
County population exposure: min(8, 1.3 × log10(population + 1))
cap at 100.
Loading & reliability
NOAA loads at startup/manual refresh; NWS refreshes every 5 minutes; CDC SVI is 2022 vintage and loads on coast changes. Large raw responses are not stored in localStorage. Only normalized state population totals use versioned 15-day entries. Cache read/write failure is non-fatal. Transient endpoint failures use bounded exponential backoff. Failure of NOAA, NWS, CDC, or local storage never blocks the other sources or UI.
Sources, cadence & attribution

National Weather Service Alerts API: coastal flood/surge products; geometry-null products resolve affected zones when needed.
