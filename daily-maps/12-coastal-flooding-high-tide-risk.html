How the score works
Derived Coastal Concern, 0–100
NOAA HTF likelihood: 0.60 × likelihood %, max 60
NWS coastal alert at station point: Warning 20, Advisory 12, Watch 8
County vulnerability: 12 × SVI percentile rank
County population exposure: min(8, 1.3 × log10(population + 1))

NOAA likelihood, flood threshold, threshold distance and update time are official/source fields. The combined score is MitchellCo screening logic, not an official forecast or evacuation guidance.
Loading & reliability
The NOAA daily-likelihood endpoint is loaded at startup and on manual refresh. CoastWatch does not invent a polling interval because NOAA's public documentation exposes a model update timestamp but does not specify a safe public refresh cadence for this product. NWS alerts refresh every 5 minutes. CDC SVI 2022 loads once per coast selection. Requests use timeouts, retry transient failures, prevent stale region responses and cache recent successful responses. If NOAA likelihoods fail, NWS alerts and SVI remain usable.
Sources, cadence & attribution
NOAA CO-OPS Derived Product API: High Tide Flooding Daily Likelihoods. Model-supplied update timestamp shown; loaded at startup/manual refresh.

National Weather Service Alerts API: Coastal Flood Warning/Advisory/Watch and Storm Surge Warning/Watch. Refreshed every 5 minutes; zone geometry resolved from official NWS zone resources where necessary.

CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index 2022: county population and overall SVI rank; static 2022 vintage, loaded once per coast selection.
