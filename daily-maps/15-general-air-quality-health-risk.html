AirWatch: General Air Quality Health Risk

How to read this map
Hazard / signal: active NWS Air Quality Alert products.
Exposure: CDC/ATSDR 2022 state population represented by counties with valid SVI values.
Consequence / vulnerability: mean county Social Vulnerability Index percentile for the state.

NWS alerts and CDC values are official source data. The MitchellCo score is a non-official triage aid and is not AQI, a pollution concentration, or a health-outcome prediction.

Derived attention score
alert hazard = 45 base + 10 if onset has arrived, max 55
exposure = min(20, 2.7 × log10(state represented population + 1))
vulnerability = 25 × mean county SVI percentile
attention = hazard + exposure + vulnerability, capped 100

If CDC context is unavailable, the map keeps official NWS alerts visible and withholds a full score rather than treating missing population/SVI as zero.
Score edge cases checked
No active alert produces no score. One future-onset alert starts at 45 hazard points. One active alert reaches 55 hazard points. Population contributes at most 20 points and cannot exceed its cap. SVI contributes 0–25. A currently active alert with large represented population and SVI 1.0 can reach the 100 cap.
Refresh & performance
NWS alert products refresh every 5 minutes, gated while hidden or offline. First paint uses one narrow active-alert query. State markers render before zone geometry or CDC context. Geometry-null NWS alerts progressively resolve official affectedZones with concurrency 3 and a 60-zone safety cap. CDC SVI is static 2022 vintage and is requested only for states implicated by active alerts. State population cache entries are versioned, state-scoped, and expire after 15 days. Cache failures are nonfatal.
Sources & attribution
National Weather Service Alerts API: active Air Quality Alert CAP products, including effective/onset/expiry, affected zones and embedded geometry when supplied. NWS documents the alerts API for redistribution and decision-support use.

CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index 2022 county layer: ST_ABBR, E_TOTPOP, and RPL_THEMES, aggregated to state context for states with active alerts.
