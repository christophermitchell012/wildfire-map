AirWatch: General Air Quality Health Risk

How to read this map
Primary: current modeled U.S. AQI from Open-Meteo/CAMS at representative state points.
Exposure: CDC/ATSDR 2022 state population, available as its own map layer.
Vulnerability: population-weighted CDC/ATSDR SVI.
NWS: active air-quality/smoke/dust/ash products. If none are active, the previous 7 days are shown as muted context.
