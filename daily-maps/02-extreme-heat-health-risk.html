What this map combines

Hazard: NWS HeatRisk and official heat alerts. Exposure: county population and population density. Vulnerability: CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index, age 65+, disability, and poverty context. Click a county for details; click anywhere else on the map to sample the current HeatRisk category.

No second composite score. NWS HeatRisk already incorporates CDC health-impact information. Adding SVI again into a derived score would double-count vulnerability and make the result look more precise than the inputs support. This map keeps the official NWS hazard value separate from CDC exposure/vulnerability context.

Refresh / vintage

NWS HeatRisk is refreshed hourly, matching the upstream hourly update cadence. NWS alerts are checked every 5 minutes, far slower than the NWS maximum recommended request frequency. CDC SVI is the 2022 national county dataset and is loaded once per selected state, then cached for the session. Polling pauses while the tab is hidden or the browser is offline, and requests are never overlapped.

Preferred layers omitted: cooling centers and hospitals are not shown because no single authoritative national browser-ready source with consistent current coverage was verified for this build. Urban tree canopy/imperviousness are also omitted rather than mixing in a large static raster whose national browser delivery path was not verified strongly enough for this run.
