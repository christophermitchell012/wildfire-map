What this map combines

Hazard: NOAA fire detections and satellite-observed smoke. Transport: NWS surface winds. Observed exposure: AirNow PM2.5. Vulnerability: CDC SVI and county population. HMS smoke can be aloft, so surface PM2.5 is kept visually distinct.

Derived smoke exposure score

Non-official score = PM + HMS + fire/wind + SVI
PM = min(50, PM2.5 AQI / 3)
HMS = 0 / 10 / 20 / 30 for none/light/medium/heavy
Fire/wind = 15 × max(0,1-distance/200 mi) × max(0,cos(Δθ)), only with fire ≤200 mi and wind station ≤150 mi
SVI = 5 × county overall SVI rank
Score capped at 100.

The score is a screening aid, not an EPA, NOAA, or NWS alert or forecast. A high score does not prove wildfire smoke caused the measured PM2.5.

Refresh / vintage

HMS smoke/fire: 30 min. NWS wind: 15 min (upstream service updates about every 10 min; individual stations may be hourly). Wind density follows NWS/MADIS scale-band layers and increases with map zoom. AirNow PM2.5 file: 30 min. CDC SVI: 2022, loaded once per state. Polling pauses while hidden/offline; requests are staggered, cached, overlap-blocked, and back off after failures.

Sources

NOAA/NESDIS HMS · HMS smoke feature layer · HMS fire feature layer · NWS/MADIS surface observations · AirNow daily-data specification · AirNow current public file · CDC/ATSDR SVI.

AirNow observations are preliminary and not regulatory AQS data. HMS smoke is satellite-observed column smoke, not a guaranteed surface concentration. No AirNow API key or NASA FIRMS MAP_KEY endpoint is used. Basemaps: USGS The National Map and OpenStreetMap; each retains its own attribution.
