Where are multiple Internet measurement probes going offline at the same time?
NetWatch fuses RIPE Atlas probe connectivity, ASN/network concentration and live RIPE RIS BGP activity. A disconnected probe is evidence of a lost measurement vantage point, not proof of a consumer Internet outage. BGP activity is independent context, not a claimed cause.

How the derived score works
Non-official outage-confidence score, 0–100.

For each ASN + ~1° geographic cell:
recent concentration = min(55, 18 × ln(1 + probes disconnected in last 24h))
recency = max(0, 25 × (1 − median disconnect age / 24h))
network concentration = min(20, 4 × distinct disconnected probes in the same ASN/cell)

The score is a triage signal only. It does not estimate affected subscribers, outage duration, or cause. A single disconnected probe is deliberately kept low-confidence.
What the fused layers mean
The strongest signal is several recently disconnected probes belonging to the same ASN and geographic area. Connected probes provide local measurement context. RIPE RIS Live separately shows whether Internet routing activity is elevated, but NetWatch does not infer that a BGP burst caused a probe disconnect. Probe coordinates are intentionally obfuscated by RIPE Atlas.
Sources, refresh & limitations
RIPE Atlas API: anonymous public probe reads. Selected-region probe snapshot every 10 minutes; connected and disconnected probes are spatially bounded. RIPE Atlas intentionally offsets probe coordinates by roughly 80–400 m for privacy.

RIPE RIS Live: anonymous browser WebSocket stream of BGP messages. NetWatch keeps only a rolling 5-minute message count as routing context and reconnects with exponential backoff.

IODA: omitted in this build because a current anonymous browser endpoint was not sufficiently verified from authoritative documentation during the build. No guessed or unofficial endpoint is used.

PeeringDB: omitted from the live map to avoid broad facility downloads and anonymous API rate pressure; RIPE Atlas ASN fields provide the network-exposure dimension directly.

The map cannot distinguish ISP failure from probe power loss, local LAN failure, maintenance, hardware failure or other causes without independent evidence.
