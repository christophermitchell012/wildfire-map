How the derived score works
Non-official outage-confidence score, 0–100.

For each ASN + ~1° cell, a single disconnected probe is capped at 20, based only on how recent the disconnect is. For clusters with 2+ probes:
recent concentration = min(55, 18 × ln(number of probes))
recency = max(0, 25 × (1 − median disconnect age / 24h))
repeat evidence = min(20, 4 × (probes − 1))

Connected probes are context only and are not required for the score.
Sources, refresh & limitations
RIPE Atlas API: anonymous probe reads. NetWatch fetches disconnected probes first with one region-bounded paginated query and refreshes every 10 minutes. Connected probes are fetched only if that layer is enabled. Only disconnected snapshots are cached in IndexedDB.

RIPE RIS Live: anonymous WebSocket stream. To avoid processing the entire global firehose, NetWatch subscribes to RRC00 only. The displayed value is therefore RRC00 UPDATE messages in the rolling previous five minutes, not all global BGP updates. UI refresh is at most every 10 seconds.

RIPE Atlas probe coordinates are intentionally obfuscated. A probe can disconnect because of ISP failure, local power/LAN failure, maintenance, hardware failure, or other causes.
