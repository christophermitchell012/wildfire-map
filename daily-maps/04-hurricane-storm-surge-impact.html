What this map combines

Hazard: NHC forecast wind fields and coastal products. Exposure: county population. Vulnerability: CDC SVI. The county layer is intentionally screened so the map is not flooded with low-relevance counties.

Derived county wind-impact screen

Non-official score = wind + 15 × SVI
County-center wind term: 0 outside forecast wind radii, 45 inside 34 kt, 65 inside 50 kt, 85 inside 64 kt. Score capped at 100.

Threshold: 55/100. At most eight counties are displayed. Zero qualifying counties is a valid result.
