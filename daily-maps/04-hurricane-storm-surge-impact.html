What this map combines

Hazard: NHC forecast cone, wind radii, coastal watches/warnings and storm-surge inundation. Exposure: county population. Vulnerability: CDC SVI. The layers stay separate so an official NHC product is never confused with a MitchellCo estimate.

Derived county wind-impact screen

Non-official score = wind + 15 × SVI
County-center wind term: 0 outside forecast wind radii, 45 inside 34 kt, 65 inside 50 kt, 85 inside 64 kt.
Score capped at 100.

This is only a screening aid. It tests the county geometry's approximate center, so edge-only impacts can be missed. It does not use the forecast cone or surge layer as probability.

Refresh / vintage

NHC tropical layers and surge: every 3 hours, matching the fastest stated NHC service cadence when a storm approaches shore; otherwise NHC typically updates every 6 hours. CDC SVI: 2022 vintage, loaded once per selected state. Polling pauses while hidden/offline; requests are cached, overlap-blocked, staggered and exponentially back off after failures.
