Sources

Basemap: loading

All four data layers are public ArcGIS feature services published in the Esri Living Atlas from federal feeds. No account, key, or token is required, and each was queried anonymously to confirm that.

Incidents and perimeters: NIFC and IRWIN interagency records, services9.arcgis.com/.../USA_Wildfires_v1
VIIRS hotspots: NASA FIRMS 375 m detections, .../Satellite_VIIRS_Thermal_Hotspots_and_Fire_Activity
MODIS hotspots: NASA FIRMS 1 km detections, last 48 hours, .../MODIS_Thermal_v1
GOES detections: NOAA NESDIS Hazard Mapping System, filtered to GOES-East and GOES-West, services2.arcgis.com/.../NOAA_Satellite_Fire_Detections_(v1)

Basemaps: six options, all served without an account or key. USGS Imagery Topo, USGS Topo, and USGS Shaded Relief come from The National Map and are US federal public domain. NASA GIBS Blue Marble is NASA EOSDIS, public domain, and caps out at zoom 8. OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap are community tile servers under their own usage policies, fine for light personal use, not for heavy traffic. Map library: Leaflet 1.9.4 from cdnjs, open source under BSD-2.

Markers are drawn with plain CSS and SVG shapes. No third-party icon files are used.

Polling: incidents and GOES every 5 minutes, perimeters and VIIRS every 10, MODIS every 15, and only for layers that are switched on. Nothing is requested while the tab is in the background or the browser is offline, one request per layer at a time, with exponential backoff after a failure. No API keys, tokens, accounts, cookies, local storage, or analytics are used anywhere on this page.
